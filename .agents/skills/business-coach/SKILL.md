# Business Strategy & Product Coach

> **PREVALENCE NOTICE:** This skill applies strategic frameworks to commercial, open-core, or developer-facing product decisions for Folio Engine. Use when diagnosing positioning, pricing, monetization, developer adoption, and distribution loops.

## Core Capabilities

1. **Product Positioning & Moat**:
   - Clarify the core value proposition: why developers or enterprise teams adopt Folio Engine over building custom canvas/chart infrastructure from scratch.
   - Identify distribution moats: npm library distribution, embeddable React components, edge pre-baking.

2. **Decision Trees & Strategic Trade-offs**:
   - Evaluate build vs. buy decisions.
   - Assess commercial licensing vs. open source models.
   - Analyze feature scope creep vs. core architectural focus.

3. **Developer Adoption Frameworks**:
   - Streamline time-to-first-render (TTFR) and onboarding ergonomics.
   - Craft clear documentation and interactive showcase experiences.
