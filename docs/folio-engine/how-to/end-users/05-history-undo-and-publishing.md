# How-To: Use History, Undo & Publish Changes Live

Every edit you make in Dev Mode lives safely in a **private draft** until you publish it. Use the floating **Dev Mode** toolbar at the bottom of the screen to undo mistakes, browse or restore past published versions, and push your approved draft live.

---

## 1. Undo & Redo Edits in Your Current Session

- **Undo**: Click **Undo** on the floating Dev Mode bar, or press **`Cmd + Z`** (Mac) / **`Ctrl + Z`** (Windows/Linux).
- **Redo**: Click **Redo**, or press **`Shift + Cmd + Z`** (Mac) / **`Ctrl + Shift + Z`** (Windows/Linux).
- Hover over **Undo** or **Redo** to preview the exact action that will be reversed or reapplied.

> [!NOTE]
> Undo/Redo tracks changes made during your active Dev Mode session and clears when you exit Dev Mode. For older published versions, use **History** below.

---

## 2. Preview or Restore Past Versions (`History`)

Click **History** on the floating Dev Mode bar to open the **History** drawer:

1. **Browse Published Revisions**: Each entry shows its change description and timestamp. The version currently visible to the public is marked **`Live`**.
2. **Restore a Past Version to Draft**: Click **Restore to Draft** next to any earlier snapshot.
   - Folio loads that snapshot into your **draft preview** only—public visitors do **not** see it yet.
   - Review the page, make any follow-up tweaks if needed, and click **Publish** to make it live.
3. **Reset to System Baseline**: Use the baseline restore button in the drawer header to load the official factory default baseline into your draft preview, then review and click **Publish** if you want to revert to defaults.

---

## 3. Publish Your Draft Live (`Publish`)

When your text, photos, and charts are ready for public visitors:

1. Click **Publish** on the floating Dev Mode bar.
2. In the **Publish Changes** dialog, describe what you updated in **What changed in this version?** (minimum **4 characters**, maximum **400 characters**).
3. Click **Publish** (or press **`Cmd + Enter`** on Mac / **`Ctrl + Enter`** on Windows/Linux).
4. Wait for the confirmation toast: **`Published changes live to portal.`** (If your internet connection dropped, it shows **`Published locally. Cloud sync pending reconnection.`**).

### Handling "Published Version Changed"
If another editor published a newer snapshot while your draft was open, **Published Version Changed** appears before overwriting their work:
- Click **Discard Draft** to drop your older local draft and reload the latest live version.
- Click **Publish Anyway** if you intentionally want your draft to become the new live version.

---

## 4. Leave Dev Mode

After publishing (when you see no remaining unpublished edits), click **Exit** on the floating Dev Mode bar and confirm **Exit** to return to the normal public view.
