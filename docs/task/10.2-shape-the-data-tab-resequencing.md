## 🎯 Purpose & Scope

Align the `Data` sub-tab with the **"Shape the Data"** stage of the mental model across all 5 chart types (`bar`, `line`, `donut`, `scatter`, `histogram`):
1. **Primary Mapping First & Open:** Every chart opens directly on its core column mapping.
2. **Data Shaping & Ordering Second:** Move `SortingAndOrderControls` out of `Appearance` into `Data` (since sorting shapes the dataset). Open Histogram's `Binning & Distribution` by default so binning is immediately discoverable.
3. **Table Orientation (Transpose) Last:** Move `Table Orientation` ("Transpose data: Swap rows and columns") from the top of `Data` to the bottom of `Data` as an optional orientation escape hatch.
4. **Checkbox Simplification in `Data`:**
   - Replace Histogram's `"Show as Percentage instead of Count"` checkbox (`StandardDataControls.tsx#L217`) with a compact `Count | Percentage` segmented choice.
   - Add a quick **All / First** helper action in `Metric Series (Y-Axis)` when there are multiple candidate columns so users don't have to uncheck 8+ columns individually.
