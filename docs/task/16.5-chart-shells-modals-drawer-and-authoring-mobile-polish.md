## Scope & Exact File Changes

### 1. Chart Shells & Empty States
- **[src/components/chart/ChartCard.module.css](file:///Users/francisdave/Documents/folio/src/components/chart/ChartCard.module.css)**:
  - Reduce horizontal/vertical padding at `@media (max-width: 640px)` to `var(--chart-pad-surface-mobile)` (`12px`), reclaiming `16px` of SVG width on `320px` viewports.
  - Ensure footer action buttons (`View Table`, `Download Data (CSV)`) have comfortable mobile spacing and tappability without wrapping awkwardly.
- **[src/components/chart/ChartRouter.module.css](file:///Users/francisdave/Documents/folio/src/components/chart/ChartRouter.module.css)**:
  - Stop hiding `.emptySubtitle` via `display: none` at `<=640px` so `"Data Distribution Pending"` retains explanatory context on mobile.
- **[src/components/chart/ChartGhostWireframe.tsx](file:///Users/francisdave/Documents/folio/src/components/chart/ChartGhostWireframe.tsx)** & **[src/components/chart/DataPendingCard.module.css](file:///Users/francisdave/Documents/folio/src/components/chart/DataPendingCard.module.css)**:
  - Replace `preserveAspectRatio="none"` on `ChartGhostWireframe` with `preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMid meet"` (or responsive aspect ratio) to eliminate ~2.5× vertical bar stretching at `320px`.
  - Reduce static `DataPendingCard` `min-height` on `<=640px` so a 2-line notice does not consume `320px` of vertical height.

### 2. `ChartTableModal`
- **[src/components/chart/ChartTableModal.tsx](file:///Users/francisdave/Documents/folio/src/components/chart/ChartTableModal.tsx)** & **[src/components/chart/ChartTableModal.module.css](file:///Users/francisdave/Documents/folio/src/components/chart/ChartTableModal.module.css)**:
