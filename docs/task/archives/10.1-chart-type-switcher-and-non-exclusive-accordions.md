## 🎯 Purpose & Scope

Improve the top-level ergonomics of Step 2 (`Chart Setup`) in [Step3ChartSetup.tsx](file:///Users/francisdave/Documents/folio/src/components/chart/drawer/panels/Step3ChartSetup.tsx) before re-sequencing the `Data` and `Appearance` panels:
1. Replace the native `<select id="cfg-chart-type">` with a token-compliant **Chart Type** segmented/button-group switcher (`bar`, `line`, `donut`, `scatter`, `histogram`) so users can see all 5 chart types at a glance and switch with one click.
2. Remove forced exclusive accordion collapsing (`name="chart-data"` / `name="chart-appearance"`) on `AccordionSection`s inside `Step3ChartSetup`, `StandardDataControls`, and `ScatterDataControls` so users can keep primary mapping and secondary controls open simultaneously without fighting auto-collapse.

---

## 🔍 Current Behavior

- `Step3ChartSetup.tsx#L134-L150` renders a standard `<select>` for `Chart Type`.
- `AccordionSection` receives `name="chart-data"` in `StandardDataControls.tsx`, `ScatterDataControls.tsx`, and `Step3ChartSetup.tsx`, and `name="chart-appearance"` in `Step3ChartSetup.tsx`. Because [AccordionSection.tsx](file:///Users/francisdave/Documents/folio/src/components/ui/AccordionSection.tsx#L41-L68) listens for `accordion-open` events matching `name`, opening any section forcibly closes all sibling sections.

---

## 🛠️ Implementation Plan

1. **Segmented Chart Type Switcher (`Step3ChartSetup.tsx` & `Step3ChartSetup.module.css`)**:
   - Build a compact 5-option segmented control (`role="radiogroup"`, `aria-label="Chart Type"`) with minimal geometric SVG icons + short labels (`Bar`, `Line`, `Donut`, `Scatter`, `Histogram`).
   - Support arrow-key navigation (`ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`) and `aria-checked`.
   - Style strictly with `src/styles/tokens.css` variables (`--folio-bg-surface`, `--folio-primary`, `--folio-border`, `--duration-fast`, `--ease-out`) and full 4-state matrix with `outline: none` on `:focus-visible`.
2. **Non-Exclusive Accordions**:
   - Omit the exclusive `name` prop (or use unique names) on `AccordionSection` instances inside `Step3ChartSetup.tsx`, `StandardDataControls.tsx`, and `ScatterDataControls.tsx` while keeping `AccordionSection` itself backward-compatible for any other callers.
