## Objective

Enable existing Drive-linked `GalleryElement` collections to automatically ingest Google Drive videos alongside photos, display restrained play/duration affordances on video cards, and play videos directly inside `SourceLightbox`.

---

## Scope & Exact File Changes

### 1. Mixed-Media Google Drive Sync
- **[src/services/drivePhotoSyncService.ts](file:///Users/francisdave/Documents/folio/src/services/drivePhotoSyncService.ts)**:
  - Update Drive API v3 `fields` to include `videoMediaMetadata(durationMillis,width,height)`.
  - Expand MIME/extension filtering to accept `video/*` (`.mp4`, `.mov`, `.webm`, `.m4v`, `.avi`, `.mkv`) alongside `image/*`.
  - Update `cleanDriveFileName` to strip common video extensions and prefixes (`VID_\d+_`, `MOV_\d+_`, `.mp4`, `.mov`, `.webm`, `.m4v`).
  - During reconciliation (`reconcileDrivePhotos`):
    - For video files, set `mediaType: 'video'`, `mimeType: file.mimeType`, `videoPreviewUrl: buildDrivePreviewUrl(file.id)`, `videoViewUrl: buildDriveViewUrl(file.id)`, and `durationMs` (parsed from `videoMediaMetadata.durationMillis` when present).
    - Use Drive's `thumbnailLink` (or edge proxy thumbnail) for `thumbnailUrl` / `photo.src` so video cards render crisp poster frames.
    - Preserve existing coordinator custom titles, order, and natural filename sorting across mixed photos and videos.
    - Legacy photo items retain full backward compatibility (`mediaType` omitted or `'image'`).

### 2. Video Badges on Cards & Thumbnail Strip
- **[src/components/source/SourceCard.tsx](file:///Users/francisdave/Documents/folio/src/components/source/SourceCard.tsx)** & **[SourceCard.module.css](file:///Users/francisdave/Documents/folio/src/components/source/SourceCard.module.css)**:
  - When `item.mediaType === 'video'` (and not an overflow `+N` tile):
    - Render a subtle, tokenized centered play badge using `PlayIcon` from `src/components/ui/Icons.tsx`.
    - When `formatVideoDuration(item.durationMs)` is truthy, render a compact bottom-right duration pill (`MM:SS`).
    - Update ARIA label to `Play video: {captionText || item.title}`.
- **[src/components/source/LightboxPhotoStrip.tsx](file:///Users/francisdave/Documents/folio/src/components/source/LightboxPhotoStrip.tsx)** & **[LightboxPhotoStrip.module.css](file:///Users/francisdave/Documents/folio/src/components/source/LightboxPhotoStrip.module.css)**:
  - Render a small `PlayIcon` indicator on video thumbnails in the bottom filmstrip.

### 3. Playable `SourceLightbox` for Mixed Slides
- **[src/components/source/SourceLightbox.tsx](file:///Users/francisdave/Documents/folio/src/components/source/SourceLightbox.tsx)** & **[SourceLightbox.module.css](file:///Users/francisdave/Documents/folio/src/components/source/SourceLightbox.module.css)**:
  - Detect `const isVideo = currentItem?.mediaType === 'video'`.
  - On video slides:
    - Render a responsive 16:9 `<iframe>` pointed at `currentItem.videoPreviewUrl || buildDrivePreviewUrl(driveFileId)` with `key={currentItem.id}` so switching slides or closing the lightbox cleanly unmounts/stops playback.
    - Disable double-click/double-tap image zoom (`isZoomed`) on video slides.
    - Keep keyboard navigation (`ArrowLeft`, `ArrowRight`, `Escape`), filmstrip selection, `"Open in Drive ↗"`, and Dev Mode `✎ Edit` working identically.
  - On photo slides: zero behavior change.

### 4. Media-Aware Copy Polish
- **[src/components/source/EditPhotoModal.tsx](file:///Users/francisdave/Documents/folio/src/components/source/EditPhotoModal.tsx)**:
  - Show `"Edit Video"` when `item.mediaType === 'video'` (else `"Edit Photo"`).
- **[src/components/source/SourceGallery.tsx](file:///Users/francisdave/Documents/folio/src/components/source/SourceGallery.tsx)**:
  - Keep photo-only galleries unchanged; use neutral `"media"` wording only when a gallery contains videos (e.g., `+N more` / aria counts).
