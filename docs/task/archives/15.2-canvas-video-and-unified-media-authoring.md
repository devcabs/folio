## Objective

Build the inline `<CanvasVideo>` component (mirroring `<CanvasPdf>`), rename the `CanvasAddBar` `Photo` option to **`Media`** (`Single Photo`, `Single Video`, `Media Gallery`), and wire `'video'` creation, editing, and deletion across the canvas authoring flow.
