## 🎯 Purpose & Scope

Align the top of the `Appearance` sub-tab with the **"Encode the Visual"** stage so users immediately see chart-defining geometry, trendlines, and scale controls when switching from `Data` to `Appearance`:
1. Move `Geometry & Visual Style` (reframed cleanly per chart type) to the **first** position in `Appearance` with `defaultOpen={true}`.
2. Split Scatter's overloaded 8-control `ScatterVisualControls` into two focused sections:
   - **Trendline & Analytics** (`defaultOpen={true}`): Trendline (Regression), Point Marker Size, LOESS Smoothness `TieredSlider`, Confidence Band, Correlation (`r`) Badge, Mean Quadrant Crosshairs.
   - **Axes & Scale** (`defaultOpen={false}`): Vertical (Y) Axis Baseline (`Auto | Zero`) and Horizontal (X) Axis Baseline (`Auto | Zero`).
3. **Checkbox Simplification in `Appearance`:**
   - Replace `Start vertical axis at zero` / `Start horizontal axis at zero` checkboxes in `LineVisualControls` and `ScatterVisualControls` with compact **`Auto | Zero`** baseline toggles.
   - In `HistogramVisualControls`, nest `Include Median & Mode` conditionally/indented under `Show Summary Statistics Card` (only shown when the Summary Statistics Card is enabled) so the panel doesn't present a 2×2 wall of 4 equal checkboxes.

---

## 🔍 Target Visual Encoding Sections by Chart Type

1. **Bar (`bar`)**: `Visual Style & Layout` *(open)* — Orientation (Vertical Columns / Horizontal Bars), Grouping Mode (Clustered / Stacked).
2. **Line (`line`)**: `Visual Style & Scale` *(open)* — Line Interpolation, Point Marker Size, Shade area under line, Start vertical axis at zero.
3. **Donut (`donut`)**: `Visual Style` *(open)* — Display Variant (Donut Ring / Solid Pie).
4. **Histogram (`histogram`)**: `Visual Style & Statistics` *(open)* — Overlay Smooth Distribution Curve, Show Mean Reference Line, Show Summary Statistics Card, Include Median & Mode.
5. **Scatter (`scatter`)**:
   - `Trendline & Analytics` *(open)* — Trendline, Point Marker Size, LOESS Smoothness (when `smooth`), Confidence Band (when trendline ≠ `none`), Correlation (`r`) Badge, Mean Quadrant Crosshairs.
   - `Axes & Scale` *(collapsed)* — Start Y Axis at Zero, Start X Axis at Zero.
